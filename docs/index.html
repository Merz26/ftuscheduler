FTU Schedule Extension

A lightweight Chrome extension designed to help students manage their schedules efficiently.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 30, 2026

FTU Schedule uses Google OAuth strictly to retrieve calendar and schedule information required for personal academic planning. We do not collect, store, or share your personal data with any external third parties.
